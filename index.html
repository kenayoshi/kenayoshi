About Me

I am an undergraduate Electrical Engineering student at Columbia University (BS, 2025–2029) minoring in Computer Science, with a cumulative GPA of 4.07/4.0. I am also a Masayoshi Son Foundation Scholar.

My primary technical interests center on embedded systems, custom PCB design, power electronics, and motor control. My work spans the full stack of hardware engineering: from bare-board bring-up, signal integrity, and analog front-end design to low-level firmware for high-throughput video streaming, sensor fusion, and multi-axis motor control.

Most recently, I researched and engineered electrical systems for vine robotics platforms at Stanford University's CHARM Lab under Prof. Allison Okamura, and I currently lead low-voltage electronics development for the Columbia University Formula SAE racing team. I am also exploring a research path toward integrated circuit (IC) design at Columbia.
