Introduction

Welcome to my website! I am an undergraduate sophomore studying Electrical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science at Columbia University (BS, 2025–2029), maintaining a cumulative GPA of 4.07 / 4.0. I am also a Masayoshi Son Foundation Scholar.

My primary technical interest and hands-on experience center on custom PCB design, embedded systems, and low-voltage hardware engineering. At Columbia, I serve as the Low Voltage Electronics Lead for the Columbia University Formula Racing team (competing in Formula SAE Electric), where I lead the PCB design, manufacturing, and validation testing of the vehicle's control electronics and safety shutdown systems.

My research interests focus on robotic control, haptic feedback, and sensor integration. In 2026, I worked as a research intern at Stanford University's CHARM Lab under Prof. Allison Okamura. There, I developed custom PCBs to drive soft vine robots, engineered compact video streaming hardware supporting wireless and Power over Ethernet (PoE) transmission, and built ultrasonic sensing front-ends for localization within confined pipe networks.

Looking forward, I am deeply curious about integrated circuit (IC) design and solid-state devices. My long-term goal is to advance the hardware technologies that enable robots to work seamlessly alongside humans and augment human capabilities.
