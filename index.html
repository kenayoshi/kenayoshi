Introduction

Hello! I am an undergraduate student studying Electrical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science at Columbia University (BS, 2025–2029), where I maintain a cumulative GPA of 4.07 / 4.0. I am also a proud Masayoshi Son Foundation Scholar.

My technical passion lies at the intersection of hardware, firmware, and physical systems — specifically in embedded systems, custom PCB design, power electronics, and high-performance motor control. My experience spans the complete hardware engineering lifecycle: schematic design, board layout, bare-board bring-up, signal integrity debugging, analog front-end design, and bare-metal / RTOS firmware development.

Recently, I conducted robotics research at Stanford University's CHARM Lab under Prof. Allison Okamura, developing electrical systems, motor controllers over CAN, and ultrasonic localization front-ends for soft vine robots. At Columbia, I lead low-voltage electronics development for the Columbia Formula SAE electric vehicle team and am actively pursuing research toward integrated circuit (IC) design.
