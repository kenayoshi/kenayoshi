PIN1 About

Ken is an undergraduate electrical engineer at Columbia University with a hands-on focus on embedded systems, PCB design, power electronics, and motor control. His work runs from bare-board PCB bring-up to firmware for camera streaming and motor control, to power-electronics subsystems for robotics platforms — most recently the electrical systems for a vine robot at Stanford's CHARM Lab. He's currently exploring a path toward IC design research at Columbia.

PIN3 Selected Projects

P01

ESP32-P4 H.264 Camera Stream

A multi-board embedded video pipeline targeting live 1080p@30fps hardware H.264 encoding over TCP, on a Waveshare ESP32-P4+C6 combo board with an OV5647 sensor — including working through an early-silicon revision and a repeatable build ritual for every new example.

ESP32-P4H.264ESP-IDF
P02

Vine Robot Electronics

Power-management and motor-control PCB for a pipe-inspection vine robot, routing CAN for motor control and I2C for sensors, alongside a custom analog front end for 1 MHz ultrasonic transducers used in underwater localization.

PCB DesignCANUltrasonic Sensing
P03

Formula SAE Shutdown-Circuit Tester

A custom PCB that automates testing of the car's shutdown circuit, replacing a manual process and cutting test time by roughly 30x — designed in Altium, simulated in LTspice, and driven by custom C firmware.

AltiumLTspiceC Firmware
P04

Dual BLDC + Stepper Control Rig

An ESP32-S3 rig driving an A4988 stepper (calibrated to 0.988 mm/rev) alongside two AK-series BLDC motors over 1 Mbit CAN, controlled through a Python/Tkinter GUI with live telemetry and integrated temperature, encoder, and FRAM sensing.

ESP32-S3CAN / TWAIPython · Tkinter
P05

48V Power Electronics Subsystem

Bus protection and motor-drive electronics for a 48V/18A system: a latching TPS16851 eFuse, a DRV8876 brushed-motor driver with regenerative-braking analysis, LMR51625 buck regulators, and evaluation of a thermal circuit breaker.

eFuseBuck ConvertersMotor Drivers
P06

Ultrasonic Time-of-Flight Ranging

A time-of-flight ranging system built around a TDC1000-C2000 EVM, with 1 MHz signal generation from an Arduino Mega timer and a diode/RC envelope detector for dual-channel echo detection — early groundwork for underwater localization on the vine robot.

TDC1000 / TDC2000Analog Front End
P07

SBC Camera Streaming Benchmarks

A comparative study of camera streaming pipelines across four single-board computers, finding WebRTC-via-mediamtx latency of ~150–200ms versus ~1.3s for RTSP, and working around ISP and pipeline bugs along the way.

Embedded LinuxWebRTCRTSP
P08

Custom ESP32-S3 PCB Bring-Up

Brought a custom ESP32-S3 board up from bare PCB, tracing a USB enumeration loop to an EN-line brownout, configuring strapping pins, and building out KiCad footprint and 3D-model workflows for future revisions.

KiCadHardware Debug

Also exploring: IC design research directions with Columbia's Analog IC group, quantum computing coursework, and a small GPT-based agent that scrapes weekly physical UI/UX developments into an SMS digest.
